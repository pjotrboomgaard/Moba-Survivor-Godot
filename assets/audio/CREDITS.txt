Attribution is not required; "Kenney.nl" is appreciated.

Packs used:
- Sci-Fi Sounds — lasers, metal impacts, force fields, explosions, thrusters, engines
  https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
  https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-sounds
- Interface Sounds — clicks, confirms, errors, glass, drops
  https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
- Digital Audio — zaps, phasers, power-ups, space-trash debris
  https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio
  https://opengameart.org/content/63-digital-sound-effects-lasers-phasers-space-etc
- RPG Audio — coin handles (gold pickup)
  https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio

Takes were trimmed and faded so combat one-shots stay short. Arena music is a lowpassed
loop of Kenney space-engine rumbles, not a chiptune melody.

See autoload/audio_service.gd for which take(s) back which in-game sound id.
Turn SFX or music off from the lobby row or the pause menu.
